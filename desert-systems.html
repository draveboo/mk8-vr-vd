Proof capsule · VKXR

A VR layer for Cemu.

VKXR is the current Desert Systems project: a focused rendering and interaction layer that brings supported Wii U titles into a headset through the tools people already use.

It demonstrates the method in practice: reduce setup friction, preserve the familiar system, add depth carefully, and keep the experience honest about what works today.
