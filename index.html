The idea

The game was never built for this.

Mario Kart 8 shipped for a console that predates consumer VR. There is no stereo mode, there never will be, and the emulator that runs it today still draws a flat screen.

VKXR sits inside Cemu's renderer and splits each frame in two, timing them to your headset. You keep the game you already own, running the way it always has — and you drive from the seat rather than watching from the couch.

Unofficial and independent. No game files, ROMs, textures or binaries are distributed with VKXR — you supply your own copy of the game and Cemu.

Why people keep it installed

Everything you want, nothing to manage

  • 01

    Depth, not a flat screen

    Every frame is split per eye and timed to your headset, so the track reads as a space you are inside rather than an image you are facing.

  • 02

    Runs inside Cemu

    VKXR is a Vulkan layer. It attaches to the emulator you already run, instead of asking you to set up a second renderer beside it.

  • 03

    Bring your own copy

    The download contains the layer and nothing else. No game files, no assets — you supply the game you already own and the emulator you already use.

  • 04

    Console controls, mapped

    Analog triggers, console-style layouts and generic gamepads all work, with bindings you can change after install without rebuilding anything.

  • 05

    Reversible in one delete

    Add the layer's folder, launch, delete the folder. No installer, no registry writes and no administrator rights.

How it works

Running in about a minute

  1. 1

    Set up Cemu and your game

    Have Cemu working with a supported title, using its Vulkan renderer, before you start.

  2. 2

    Add the VKXR layer

    Copy the layer into place. No installer, no administrator rights, nothing written elsewhere.

  3. 3

    Launch in VR

    Start the game with your headset connected. The layer picks up the renderer and presents in stereo.

Questions

Before you download

Do I need to own the game?

Yes. VKXR distributes no game files, ROMs, textures or assets of any kind. You supply your own copy of the game and your own Cemu install.

Which games are supported?

Mario Kart 8 is supported today. Super Mario 3D World is in development. Support is written per title, so other Wii U games will not work yet.

Do I need Cemu first?

Yes. VKXR is a layer for Cemu and does nothing on its own — it needs a working Cemu install with a supported title running on the Vulkan renderer.

Is this official or endorsed?

No. It is an independent, unofficial, non-commercial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Nintendo, Cemu, or any headset or runtime vendor.

Is it free?

Yes. It is open source with no account, no subscription and no telemetry.

Will it slow the game down?

It is built to stay under roughly two percent of your processor, and it works inside the frame Cemu already renders rather than alongside it.

Does it work with my headset?

If your headset runs on Windows through an OpenXR runtime, VKXR can present to it — including the runtimes most people already have installed.

Will this get me banned online?

Treat online play as unsupported and at your own risk. The game is being modified at runtime, and nobody can guarantee how an online service will react to that. If you care about your online standing, play offline.

How do I remove it?

Delete the layer's folder. That is the whole process — nothing is written to the registry or anywhere else on your system.
